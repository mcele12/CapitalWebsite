About Our State

The state of Hawaii, which officially achieved statehood in 1959, features its capital city, Honolulu, located on the highly populated island of Oahu. Considered a major urban area, Honolulu encompasses multiple diverse districts, including the world-famous Waikiki Beach and resort strip. From an economic perspective, the city's household income is divided into three main tiers: 20% of households represent the high-income tier earning $200,000 or more, 58.2% make up the middle-income tier earning between $50,000 and $199,999 (with a median of $106,195), and the remaining 21.8% occupy the lower-income tier earning under $50,000.
